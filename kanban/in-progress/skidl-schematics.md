### skidl-schematics — readable schematics from skidl
Turns skidl circuits into hierarchical KiCad schematics a person can read, with one sheet per subcircuit and reference panels from the datasheets. First customer: mubone. A byproduct tool the company could ship later.

Part of [[sloptaumatons.com]]. State: `projects/skidl-schematics/status.md`.
